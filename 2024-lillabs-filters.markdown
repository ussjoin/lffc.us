## LilLabs Wildfire Smoke Filtration - Proposed Budget

## Overview

Given standard aphorisms about necessity and invention, the early part of the COVID pandemic spurred the creation of the [Corsi-Rosenthal Box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corsi%E2%80%93Rosenthal_Box) for air filtration. Subsequent designs have come along to optimize for various needs, including portability. For the target community of LilLabs, being able to power a wildfire smoke filter using USB power is key; this allows people to use a USB power bank, wall charger, or anything else that can provide USB power to run the filter.

I had intended to recommend the purchase of filters to build the [USB-powered Mini CR design](https://www.texairfilters.com/corsi-rosenthal-box-modular-mini-small-quiet-and-effective/), using [$7.91 filters](https://filterking.com/air-filter-sizes/10x10x2) plus USB fans for an approximate cost/unit of $50. However, a friend has used and recommends [these premade HEPA-rated air filters](https://mookashops.com/collections/air-purifier/products/air-purifiers-for-bedroom-home-mooka-hepa-h13-filter-air-purifier-with-usb-cable-for-smokers-pollen-pets-dust-odors-in-office-car-300-sq-ft), which are designed for the approximate same size of room, are pre-made commercial products (with replacement air filters available), and are still USB-powered---all for a lower total price.
