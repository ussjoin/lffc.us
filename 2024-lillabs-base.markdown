## LilLabs $500 Grant - Proposed Budget

I've chosen to focus on lead testing for the UW-funded supplies, for the following reasons:

* It appears that other contaminants in soil in South Seattle (e.g., benzene, petrochemicals) go along with lead, so the presence of lead can serve as a leading indicator.
* A significant number of children in this area are testing positive for lead poisoning, and community institutions, such as the [Odessa Brown Children's Clinic](https://www.seattlechildrens.org/clinics/odessa-brown/), are using lead testing as an indicator of whether well-child visits are sufficiently meeting patient needs. (Pendleton, S. (ARNP, FNP-C), "OBCC Well Child SmartSet and Notes Update", 2023.)
* Lead testing is relatively easy to perform outside a lab, compared with other contaminants.

Hence, it's a place to start.
