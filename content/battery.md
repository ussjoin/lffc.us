Hello! You've come to this page from one of our loaner USB batteries. We're happy to provide loaner USB batteries so that you can recharge your phone or other mobile devices *without* having to stand around and wait. Our batteries have on-board USB-C, Micro-USB, and Lightning cables so that they can charge most mobile devices, but they also have USB ports on board to charge things with other cables.

**When the battery is discharged, please return it to us so we can recharge it. You are welcome to take another at that time!**

Our batteries are *loans*, not gifts. They are not for sale or barter, nor are they to be given away. We request that you bring them back to us at

7014 42nd Ave S
Seattle, WA 98118

We're right next to the Othello stop on Link.

If you can't get the battery back to us, but you can let us know where it is so we can get it, please do so; drop us an email at [info@lffc.us](mailto:info@lffc.us) and we'll work out picking it up.

Our batteries are uniquely tagged so we can identify them and keep an inventory of them.
