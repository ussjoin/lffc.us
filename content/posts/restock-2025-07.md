Welcome to the Restock!

### Updates and Changes

We've spent a bunch of time in the past month preparing for a whole new LFFC roof and cabinet. Now, this might strike you as controversial, but it rains _a lot_ in Seattle--and while we thought we had that handled with the roof, a year's water infiltration into the cabinet materials (which turned out to be MDF, whoops) proved us wrong. We've got new plans, a new (larger) roof, and a new (more waterproof) cabinet; the whole thing will fit in the same footprint, but have slightly more capacity and be quite a lot more waterproof. New photos coming soon!

### Monthly Usage

This month, we had fourteen outgoing completed calls made from the [Futel](https://futel.net/) phone.

This month, we distributed:

* 198 pairs of thermal socks
* 1080 Band-Aids
* 55 CoBan rolls
* 35 ABD gauze pads, and 46 non-adherent pads
* 375 nitrile gloves
* 76 packets of Vaseline
* 223 masks (N95 and KF94)
* 109 doses of ibuprofen
* 63 pairs of earplugs
* 54 ponchos
* 80 pencils
* 67 bars of soap
* 125 toothbrushes
* 81 tubes of toothpaste 
* 16 tampons
* 42 menstrual pads (across all sizes)
* 221 condoms, 6 female condoms, and 182 lubricant packets
* 34 boxes (68 doses) of naloxone

Our [COGGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cost_of_goods_sold) for this month is $702.63, not including naloxone, condoms, or lubricants (all of which are provided by outside groups at no cost to us).

We received donations this month from [Harley LaRoux](https://www.harleylaroux.com/) and Andrew Smart, as well as one from an anonymous donor.
