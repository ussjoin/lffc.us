Welcome to the Restock!

### Updates and Changes

Not so many changes this month. We got hats and gloves back into the cabinets, and we continued to see increased usage, even with a brief unavoidable pause in restocking for much of a week. It's clear there is a huge amount of unmet need---and as Bruce Harrell and Ann Davison continue to weaponize the police against the houseless, I have no doubt that needs will increase going forward.

### Monthly Usage

This month, we distributed:

* 168 pairs of thermal socks
* 75 pairs of thermal gloves
* 69 warm hats
* 820 Band-Aids
* 61 CoBan rolls
* 34 gauze pads
* 346 nitrile gloves
* 182 masks (N95 and KF94)
* 16 tubes of sunscreen (still useful in the winter!)
* 123 doses of ibuprofen
* 45 pairs of earplugs
* 214 pairs of handwarmers
* 98 ponchos
* 45 pencils
* 38 bars of soap
* 97 toothbrushes
* 64 tubes of toothpaste
* 120 tampons
* 29 menstrual pads (across all sizes)
* 121 condoms, and 161 lubricant packets
* 24 soil lead tests, and three ten-packs of object lead tests
* 30 boxes (60 doses) of naloxone

Our [COGGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cost_of_goods_sold) for this month is $949.23, not including naloxone, condoms, lubricants, or lead tests (all of which are provided by outside groups at no cost to us).

We received two donations this month from [Harley LaRoux](https://www.harleylaroux.com/) and Erin Condit-Bergren. Thank you so much for your generosity!

### 2024 Summary

We started on Labor Day Weekend 2024, so essentially September 1. In four continuous months of operation, we've given away in total:

* 620 pairs of socks
* 171 pairs of warm gloves, and 69 hats
* 460 condoms, and 334 packets of lubricant
* 75 boxes of naloxone (150 doses)
* 1,760 Band-Aids
* 114 CoBan rolls
* 97 gauze pads
* 911 nitrile gloves
* 342 masks
* 48 tubes of sunscreen
* 278 doses of ibuprofen
* 493 pairs of handwarmers
* 169 ponchos
* 101 pencils
* 101 bars of soap
* 161 toothbrushes, and 107 tubes of toothpaste
* 439 period products of various sorts

The COGGA in this period has been $2,345.82.

Not bad for a cabinet on a driveway.
