Welcome to the Restock!

### Updates and Changes

The only major change this month: we've expanded our zine selection again! We now include several excellent zines from the [Reproductive Health Access Project](https://www.reproductiveaccess.org/) and [Fenestrate Press](https://www.fenestrate.net/) in addition to our existing libraries.


### Monthly Usage

This month, we had twelve outgoing completed calls made from the [Futel](https://futel.net/) phone.

This month, we distributed:

* 192 pairs of thermal socks
* 1020 Band-Aids
* 45 CoBan rolls
* 34 ABD gauze pads, and 49 non-adherent pads
* 450 nitrile gloves
* 67 packets of Vaseline
* 206 masks (N95 and KF94)
* 75 doses of ibuprofen
* 62 pairs of earplugs
* 58 ponchos
* 37 pencils
* 28 bars of soap (yes, all three of those really were 55, random numbers are random)
* 134 toothbrushes
* 71 tubes of toothpaste 
* 119 tampons
* 149 menstrual pads (across all sizes)
* 253 condoms, 17 female condoms, and 141 lubricant packets
* 27 boxes (54 doses) of naloxone

Our [COGGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cost_of_goods_sold) for this month is $635.34, not including naloxone, condoms, or lubricants (all of which are provided by outside groups at no cost to us).

We received donations this month from [Harley LaRoux](https://www.harleylaroux.com/), as well as one from an anonymous donor. We also have had significant help restocking from a friend of LFFC who prefers to "keep a low profile" but has been _so_ helpful filling in. Thank you so much for your generosity!

### Conclusion

That's it for this month's Restock! If there's more information you'd like to know, please feel free to use our [Suggestions Form](/suggestions/) or email us at [info@lffc.us](mailto:info@lffc.us).
