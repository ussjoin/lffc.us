Welcome to the Restock!

### Updates and Changes

It's finally getting cold at night, so we started putting out warm gloves, hats, and handwarmers this past week.

We have taken written (and electronic) comments at LFFC since our first day, and many of the things we distribute were originally suggested by visitors in the comments. We've been meaning to put them all online, and we've [finally done so](https://lffc.us/comments/). We get heartwarming comments from time to time, and this month received a [lovely one from someone who was planning to use our phone as the beginning of a chance in circumstances](https://lffc.us/comments/2026-10/0001-edited.png). Going forward, we'll continue to post all our comments on the [comments page](https://lffc.us/comments/) as they get scanned, and list any especially interesting ones here in the Restock.
